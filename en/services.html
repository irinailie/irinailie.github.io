ZEST Collective is an architecture studio in Bucharest. We design and coordinate interior design projects through all stages: concept, building permits, technical detailing to materalization.

SERVICES

INTERIOR DESIGN

Workspace Design - projects driven by our “design the workday, not the workspace” approach. Our designs are informed by the latest research in workspace wellbeing and office productivity

Commercial Design - interior design projects for spaces that work with the public, from restaurants to retail shops.

For any the projects you decide to work with us for, you can access our expertise from the concept stage until the first use.

VISUALISATION AND PLANNING TECHNOLOGIES

Our studio employs BIM (Building Information Modeling) software and digital tools that facilitate an efficient workflow and a better collaboration with both engineers and builders.

Photorealistic visualisations just got better. You can now experience our design proposals in Virtual Reality and feel more confident with the decisions you make. Would you like to see your future project in real size before the building phase? Contact us.
